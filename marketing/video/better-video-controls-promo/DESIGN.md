# Better Video Controls promo

## Style Prompt
Punchy, dark product demonstration, using the existing blue and violet app icon as the visual anchor. The actual extension operating on a local HTML5 video is the hero. Large, tight editorial captions on the left, a generously sized browser recording on the right. Open on the benefit and gesture, show forward and backward seeking, fast seeking and precision, then hold a direct install call to action. Designed to be understood with sound muted.

## Colors
- #10101B — canvas
- #F6F5FF — primary text
- #B8B6CF — secondary text
- #B49AFF — violet emphasis derived from app icon
- #6BA9FF — gesture highlight derived from app icon

## Typography
Arial bold for benefit statements, regular Arial for explanation; monospace for keyboard keys and step numbers. The plain, familiar typography leaves attention on the interaction.

## Motion
Short 0.3–0.5 second entrances. Horizontal push transitions echo horizontal seeking. Gesture dots travel sideways in sync with real wheel actions. No slow logo intro. Final CTA holds for at least four seconds.

## What NOT to Do
- No fabricated player behavior or fake performance measurements.
- No legacy Media Flow Seek branding.
- No busy gradients behind copy or tiny tutorial captions.
- No implication of platform endorsement.
- Do not color-grade extension UI.
